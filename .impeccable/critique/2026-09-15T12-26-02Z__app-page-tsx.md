## Overall Impression

The content is honest and specific — real reviews, real capacities, a deliberate non-default color/type system — but two things undercut it before a visitor gets anywhere: a CSS cascade bug silently defeats every "white text on dark background" override (including the hero headline itself), and the page's structure is a generic marketing-page template with nothing that visually says "Puy du Fou" or "kids' outdoor games" beyond the words. The single biggest opportunity: fix the contrast bug (it's one CSS ordering fix with sitewide impact) and then give the page one or two moments — a distance-to-park callout, a photo of the pétanque/molkky setup the reviews mention — that only this business could show.

## What's Working

1. **Reviews are real, specific, and server-rendered directly beneath the hero** — named reviewers, real dates, quotes naming exact amenities a Puy du Fou family cares about. Correct placement, correct content, and (per CLAUDE.md) intentionally shipped without a fabricated `reviewRating`.
2. **Content discipline.** `lib/data/site-images.ts` explicitly avoids claiming a photo "is" a specific gîte when it isn't confirmed, and gîte/USP copy is traceable to real prior-site content rather than invented.
3. **A genuine, non-default color/type system** (forest green + terracotta, Fraunces display serif) that reads as a considered brand choice, not a Tailwind default.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Hero H1 and the "Notre territoire à découvrir" H2 render in near-black text on dark backgrounds, despite `text-white` in the JSX.**
- Why it matters: the H1 is the first thing every visitor reads, and CLAUDE.md names Lighthouse Accessibility 100/100 and contrast as explicit, non-negotiable audit fixes for this rebuild — this reproduces the exact class of defect the rebuild exists to fix. The runtime detector confirms it with real ratios: hero subhead `1.1:1` and H2 `1.4:1` against a `4.5:1`/`3:1` requirement.
- Root cause (verified in source): `app/globals.css` defines `.h1`, `.h2`, `.btn-outline` etc. as plain CSS rules placed *after* `@tailwind utilities;`, not inside `@layer components`. Equal specificity + later source order means these classes silently beat utility overrides like `text-white` everywhere they're used on a dark section — the hero H1, the "Notre territoire" H2, and the WhatsApp button text all render in their default dark colors.
- Fix: wrap the custom classes in `globals.css` inside `@layer components { ... }` (or move the block above `@tailwind utilities;`) so utility overrides reliably win, then re-check every dark-background section.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The page ends on a CTA-less, flat "Contactez-nous" block that the footer duplicates seconds later in scroll.**
- Why it matters: violates the peak-end rule — the last impression before a booking decision is administrative boilerplate, not a confident close, and the immediate address/phone/email repeat adds scroll with zero new information.
- Fix: precede or replace the in-page `#contact` block with a closing CTA ("Prêt pour votre séjour au Puy du Fou ? Réservez maintenant" + button); drop the redundant contact text or give it something the footer lacks (a map, a contact form).
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Three of four gîte cards (A, B, D) share identical copy, and Gîte D's card photo is a mirrored duplicate of Gîte A's.**
- Why it matters: at the exact moment a parent needs to differentiate 4 options, 3 cards offer nothing to distinguish and one photo is a visibly flipped repeat — this reads as a bug rather than "content not yet available," eroding trust right before the page's highest-stakes click.
- Fix: vary photo crop/angle more distinctly per card at minimum; add one real distinguishing detail per gîte even while layout stays identical. This is a known open item in CLAUDE.md (photos not yet identified per gîte) — flag to Nicolas rather than inventing a distinction.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Desktop header exposes 8 simultaneous top-level choices (7 nav links + "Réserver") with no active-page indicator, and the eyebrow-then-heading pattern repeats identically across all 5 sections.**
- Why it matters: exceeds the ~5-item working-memory guideline for primary nav with no "you are here" cue; and the detector-confirmed 5x repetition of the same kicker/heading structure is exactly what makes the page read as templated rather than authored for this product (see Design Specificity above).
- Fix: demote "Découvrir la région" and "Services" to a lighter secondary treatment or footer-only placement; add active-route styling. Separately, vary at least one section's heading treatment (image-led, stat callout, etc.) so not every section reads identically.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P3] "Séjour 100% autonome" USP text ("Commandes en ligne, paiements sécurisés, livraisons au gîte") implies a live online-ordering flow that doesn't exist in this build (shop is explicitly out of scope per CLAUDE.md).**
- Why it matters: small expectation mismatch right in the persuasion section — a visitor primed to expect online ordering finds nothing.
- Fix: soften to describe the service without implying a live checkout ("produits locaux livrés directement au gîte, sur simple demande") until the shop ships.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`
